# Hiri Divers Lanka — Diving Company Website Development

I worked with Hiri Divers Lanka to build a full website for their diving business. The client wanted the new site to follow the same structure and visual style as one of their existing websites, so the design direction was already clear. Once the content was delivered, I started development using Next.js with Tailwind CSS for styling.

The site was deployed on Netlify, and the domain was purchased through register.lk. I configured DNS and nameservers to connect the domain with Netlify and handled all record management during setup.

One of the main requests from the client was a contact form that sends submissions directly to email. After comparing different options, I used Resend for form handling and email delivery and walked the client through the pros and cons of each alternative before final approval.

All CI/CD pipelines were configured from the beginning, so every update moves to production smoothly without manual deployment. The website is now live and ranking at the top of Google search results for its relevant keywords.

## Technical summary

- Framework: Next.js
- Styling: Tailwind CSS
- Hosting & Deployment: Netlify
- Domain & DNS: register.lk + Netlify nameservers
- Contact Form Email Delivery: Resend
- CI/CD: Fully automated from Git to production
- SEO & Performance optimized
