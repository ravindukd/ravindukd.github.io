# Sarvaposha.lk - E Commerce / Product Catalog Website Development

I built the website for Sarvaposha.lk based on a design sample provided by the client. They supplied the content and asked for a simple and clear product display without a full cart or checkout system. Instead, each product opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message containing the product ID to match their ordering flow.

The site uses Next.js with Supabase as the backend and is hosted on Netlify. The domain was configured through register.lk and DNS pointed to Netlify.

## Technical summary

- Framework: Next.js
- Backend: Supabase
- Hosting & Deployment: Netlify
- Domain & DNS: register.lk + Netlify nameservers
- WhatsApp integration for product inquiries
- CI/CD pipelines configured from day one
