# All Bits of ICT — Online Notes & Tools for A/L ICT

This project aims to make A/L ICT learning more organized and less stressful for students. Many learners struggle not because of lack of effort, but because content is scattered, tools are separate, and progress is hard to measure. This platform brings notes, tools, and study paths together in one place so students can learn with clarity and keep track of what they have done or what comes next.

It includes structured digital notes, mindmaps, past papers, and step-by-step study paths. Students can switch between different learning modes such as first-time study, revision, or past paper practice, and keep separate progress for each track. They can also bookmark, mark lessons as completed, and return to what they were last working on.

To support practical learning, the platform also includes simulators and calculators such as the Fetch-Decode-Execute Cycle Simulator, Number System Converter, Karnaugh Map tool, SQL Sandbox, and more. These are meant to help students understand concepts, not just memorize theory.

The content is still being built, and many chapters and tools are in progress. My goal is not to replace teachers or textbooks, but to give students a simple and clear space to learn ICT at their own pace, without losing track of what they have done or what comes next.

This project will keep improving based on real feedback from students and teachers. If you are interested, open to testing, or willing to share suggestions, I would truly appreciate it. This is a long-term effort, and I hope it grows into something genuinely helpful for the ICT learning community.
