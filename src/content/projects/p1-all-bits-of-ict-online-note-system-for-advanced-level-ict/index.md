# All Bits of ICT — Online Notes & Tools for A/L ICT

This project aims to make A/L ICT learning more organized and less stressful for students. Many learners struggle not because of lack of effort, but because content is scattered, tools are separate, and progress is hard to measure. This platform brings notes, tools, and study paths together in one place so students can learn with clarity and keep track of what they have done.

## Overview

- Structured digital notes and mindmaps covering A/L ICT syllabus topics.
- Step-by-step study paths that guide learners from first-time study to revision and past-paper practice.
- Progress tracking that is kept separate per learning mode so students can switch modes without losing context.

## Features

- Bookmark lessons, mark lessons as completed, and quickly return to the last active lesson.
- Multiple learning modes: first-time study, revision, and past-paper practice — each with independent progress tracking.
- Mindmaps and visual summaries to help students connect concepts instead of memorising isolated facts.

## Tools & Simulators

To support practical understanding (not rote learning), the platform includes interactive tools such as:

- Fetch-Decode-Execute Cycle Simulator
- Number System Converter
- Karnaugh Map tool
- SQL Sandbox
- Other calculators and small simulators to practice key ICT concepts

These are designed to demonstrate concepts step-by-step and let students experiment with examples.

## Progress & Tracking

Students can:

- Track completion per lesson and per learning mode
- See study progress at a glance
- Resume where they left off across devices

The goal is to make learning measurable and motivating, not punitive.

## Current Status

Content and tools are actively being built. Many chapters and simulators are works in progress. The platform is intended to evolve with feedback from students and teachers.
